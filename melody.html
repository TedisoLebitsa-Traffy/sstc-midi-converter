Melody to MIDI

Paste a melody, hear it, then download MIDI or an ESP32 LED-test sketch.

Use C4:1 D4:0.5 R:0.5 E4:2 for note:beats, with R for a rest. You can also paste the Happy Birthday Arduino sketch or its {"G4", 1} song list.

ESP32 LED-test sketch

For a classic ESP32-WROOM-32 DevKit, connect GPIO 25 through a 330 Ω resistor to the LED’s long leg, and the short leg to GND. Keep the coil disconnected. The LED shows the rhythm, and the Serial Monitor at 115200 baud shows the notes.
